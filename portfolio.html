Training Education

Beeckestijn Business School

As a lecturer at Beeckestijn Business School, I delivered a practice-driven training focused on AI, vibecoding, and automations for working professionals.

Rather than centering on technology itself, the sessions emphasized practical application in everyday professional contexts. The training combined strategic insight with hands-on experimentation. Participants learned how vibecoding enables rapid translation of ideas into functional prototypes, and how smart automations can eliminate repetitive tasks, creating space for creativity and high-value decision-making. AI was positioned as an extension of human thinking — accessible, actionable, and tailored to real organizational challenges.
